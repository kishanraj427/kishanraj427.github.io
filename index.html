Hi! I'm Raj Kishan 🙋

He/His

Wow! A whole page just about me?

I'm a senior software engineer, currently at Eicore, building an AI-powered web SaaS product for the insurance sector. Most of my time goes on working out how to put artificial intelligence into production without giving up performance or code quality.

I wrote my 'Hello World' at 18 and fell in love with what a few lines of code could do. That grew into a real interest in full-stack development, covering mobile, web and the backend behind both. It also left me caring about system design and real production bottlenecks, not just shipping code that runs.

My early background is in Cyber Security, and it stuck. I approach development security-first and at a systems level, which tends to show up as fewer surprises later.

When I'm not coding, you can find me exploring new places or wandering through nature's beauty. If you want to talk finance, tech or travel, shoot me an email.

P.S. Feel free to send nature pictures.
